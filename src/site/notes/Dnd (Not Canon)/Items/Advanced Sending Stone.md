**The Advanced Sending Stone** is a sending stone invented by [[Dnd (Not Canon)/PCs/Skink\|Skink]] that sends messages to his past self. However, he has been getting messages from someone unfamiliar.

The person speaking to him is [[Dnd (Not Canon)/The Entity\|The Entity]] that exists in the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Shadowlands\|Shadowlands]]. Similarly to how [[People/Shadowy Beasts/Battery\|Battery]] was formed, [[Dnd (Not Canon)/The Entity\|The Entity]] is formed through Skinks great talent in both intellectualism and creativity. As the campaign goes on, [[Dnd (Not Canon)/The Entity\|The Entity]] becomes more complex.


“Dream for me, and find me, in the bones of the Earth.” - Said to Skink on [[Dnd (Not Canon)/Notes/2025-08-10 Summary\|2025-08-10 Summary]]

[[Dnd (Not Canon)/The Entity\|The Entity]] can be found in the [[Dnd (Not Canon)/Court of Thrones\|Court of Thrones]] after the illusion from [[Dnd (Not Canon)/Mizzette\|Mizzette]] dissipates and a tear into the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Shadowlands\|Shadowlands]] is created.
