**Mizzette** (She/Her/Hers) is a young Lizardfolk that got lost in the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Shadowlands\|Shadowlands]] and founded the [[Dnd (Not Canon)/Court of Thrones\|Court of Thrones]]


She relit [[Dnd (Not Canon)/The Lamplighter\|The Lamplighter]] 


Mizzette became a construct of the Shadowlands in the time she was stuck there. Her dreams become her reality and she begins to break apart as she is disillusioned. She is full of stuffing instead of guts.
