Cass started to go mad in his and [[Dnd (Not Canon)/PCs/Skink\|Skink]]'s partnership. In their research of making the [[Dnd (Not Canon)/Items/Advanced Sending Stone\|Advanced Sending Stone]], he had seen past the veil into the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Shadowlands\|Shadowlands]] and was frightened. Their invention was creating holes in the very fabric of the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Mortal Realm\|Mortal Realm]] 

"Not quite of the material world, or even that of magic, but the jelly in between that holds it all together."

Because the Shadowlands is the realm of dreams and thought, [[Dnd (Not Canon)/The Entity\|The Entity]] was able to sense Cass's plan to destroy their work, so it spoke to Skink, mimicking his voice, telling him to kill his partner. And it worked.
