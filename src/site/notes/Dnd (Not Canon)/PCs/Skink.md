Played by Siah



What is a moment from your past where you were in love?:
Working on the [[Dnd (Not Canon)/Items/Advanced Sending Stone\|Advanced Sending Stone]], I started receiving anonymous messages from the future, I had no clue who the person on the other end of the sending stone was but they knew me perfectly. They understood me better than anyone. Stein Condoriano fell in love with the messages, not understanding them or who they were from

What is a moment from your past where you were angry?:
Stein got a message from the sending stone. He couldn't believe the message. It was a command that no person could comprehend. "Your lab partner intends to kill you and steal your research. Kill them first or you will die." A swirl of emotions demand a sacrifice from him. He goes from sorrow to anger when he realizes who was on the other end of the sending stone. A message simply reads "warn yourself of Sebastian's plot to kill you to complete the loop." He told himself to kill his own lab partner and best friend.

Tied to the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Shadowlands\|Shadowlands]]
With how powerful his ideas and creativity was toward his projects, a shapeless entity was formed, and when he made his Sending Stone, it found a way to communicate with him. The longer the campaign goes, and the brighter his ideas are, the more sophisticated the entity becomes.
