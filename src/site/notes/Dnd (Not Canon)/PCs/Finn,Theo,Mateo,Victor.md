Played by Kris. Changes age (and name) at different times. Name was taken by the fae 3 whole times


Associated with the [[Places/Territories/Oasis/Rumor Mill\|Rumor Mill]], 


Old friend, [[Dnd (Not Canon)/Winston\|Winston]] (Business loving businessmen) got involved with the Rumor Mill and came back to the mortal realm to get some scholarly information on mortal magic. Victor helped and something happened, resulting in the destruction of important documents. Winston has had a vendetta ever since.

The incident was from Victor accidentally spilling an experimental concoction over a bunch of books. It also splashed onto Winston's arm and burned the feathers off

After the argument, Winston turned around and started a rumor on Victor, bringing many fae to start tormenting him


Things Victor knows of the Fae realm:

You never visited the faerie realm itself, but you lived vicariously through Winston's experience. He's told you before about his hometown, Rhasa, capitol of the [[Places/Territories/Oasis/Oasis\|Oasis]]. There is a strong culture that's centered around [[Places/Territories/Oasis/The Bazaar\|The Bazaar]]. Many artisans in various crafts will sell their work here. It is also popular for a variety of street food. Winston would talk about his experience in vivid tales, yet he would never offer to bring you... in fact, it seemed like he'd shut down the entire idea whenever you'd brought it up.

Winston's research was funded by the Rumor Mill. He would rarely talk about this place, but there were times where you felt this deal was more dangerous than how it appeared on the surface.

You'd also learned a little bit about the god [[People/Gods/Bahamut\|Bahamut]]], The Father Below, The Resting One. Many fae folk who follow the old gods believe that the faerielands were built around the cosmic egg of Bahamut, and that all things came to be because he dreamed. Winston was never super religious, but his father gifted him a talisman of Bahamut that he wore everywhere.
