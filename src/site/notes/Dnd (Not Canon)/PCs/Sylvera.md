Played by Jo


Magic smells like Rose and Bergamot 




A pitch: After running away from your village, you came across a satyr in the [[Places/Territories/Grasslands\|Grasslands]]. At a young age, you made a bargain with him: So long as you don't leave the Grasslands, you will find protection from the volatile magic of the Faerie Realm. From then on, you lived with him and his nephew [[People/Dionysus\|Dionysus]]. 

Growing up, you heard many stories about the fire of the [[Places/Territories/Meadows\|Meadows]]. It had been a fresh scar to many of the people you were around. You were told tales about the Fairchild family, the royal family of the Meadows. How out of nowhere, in an instant, the castle was burnt to rubble, and the fire continues to spread throughout the land. The magic then became weaker, and many of the residents had to find somewhere else to live.

Due to the bargain you made, you were never allowed to explore this mysterious land you'd heard so much about. There were many times where this deal was stifling, but as you grew up, you better understood where your father was coming from. He'd lost his family in that fire, and he grew so afraid of you meeting the same fate.

The Grasslands mostly consists of farmland and a few sparce towns. It was hardly the place for someone who craves adventure to stay forever. Occasionally, you would go into town to steal from shops. This never scratched the itch. One day, you decided it was worth the risk of leaving town, and you soon found yourself in the [[Dnd (Not Canon)/Festival of Ember and Feathers\|Festival of Ember and Feathers]] (The Carnival)
