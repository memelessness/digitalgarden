Played by Lori

Tied to the [[Places/Territories/Meadows\|Meadows]]

Kyiidd was baby swapped by two faeries that ended up raising him on their own. His father was a falconer and worked for the royal guard. 

After the Meadows burnt down, he ran to the [[Places/Territories/Forest\|Forest]] and used his survival skills to hide in the shadows. Something happened and it resulted in him losing his memory and returning to the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Mortal Realm\|Mortal Realm]].


Kyiidd has a carved talisman shaped like a falcon. It smells faintly like lavender (father's magic).


Kyiidd's magic smells like Daffodils
