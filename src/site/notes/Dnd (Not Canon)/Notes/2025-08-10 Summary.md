On your way to the big top, you came across a crying child being consoled by a High Faerie man. Her mother is missing, you have deduced that she has been taken.

The faerie’s name is Eowynn. He is a taller man with an otherworldly presence that follows him. Despite this, he still has wrinkles in the corners of his eyes. He is in charge of the Storytime Corner, and has assured you that they do not stamp your Ticket for attendance and would be more than happy to talk after the next storytime. 

At the Big Top, you saw Lucien Yarrow’s show where he displayed his magical whistle that pierces the veil between realms. You’ve also been introduced to Viktor Yarrow (different to Victor), his husband and show partner. At the end of the show, the carnival was brought back in time, giving you another day at the festival.

After the show, you went to Lucien and Viktor’s caravan. You looked into the whistle, noticing that not only is it not magical, but he keeps many of them.

After leaving the caravan, you went back to the Big Top and spoke with Rufus, a Bug Bear in charge of cleaning up the stage. You learned from him that the entrance to the Fae Wilds is found at the Ice Rink of Illusions, where you saw Ruben fall through the ice, though he doesn’t remember the rhyme.

Another one of you has seen The Child (Mizzette)
