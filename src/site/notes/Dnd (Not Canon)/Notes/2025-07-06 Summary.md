In your quest for finding access to the faerie realm, you continue to make your way through the Festival of Ember and Feathers. You decided the next place to look was the Pixie Kingdom, a smaller circus found in the knothole of a tree. 

While exploring the Pixie Kingdom, you came across a hamster named Byskutt. He told you about a few happenings in the festival, including Lucien Yarrow’s whistle that is said to have the power to overcome the veil between worlds. He also mentioned a candleman that he is concerned about yet isn’t allowed to discuss.

Another one of you has also seen The Lamplighter
