You've just stepped into the Faerielands , greeted with tall trees that stretch out to the sky, leaves intermingling with a thick fog.

As you look around, you notice the trees have faces, all with different expressions, many with multiple faces stacked on each other. Notable faces you saw was one of a sad woman, a much smaller face pressed to her cheek. Another was of a smug man, deep cuts buried in its eyes.

You found a path, and followed it to see three teenagers playing music, dancing and stomping around the fire. They noticed you, smothering the fire before walking away.

You decided to set up camp, enjoying some good beer and meal. After camp was set up and it got dark, you worked in shifts to keep a lookout.

Most of you had seen the fox girl come back, mask still covering her face as she watches you. In the morning, when she believes you're distracted, she breaches the threshold of your camp to reclaim her drum mallet. Sylvera, who was guarding the camp at the time, saw her and transformed into a fox to try to give her some ease.

The girl was somewhat relieved by this, but was still very guarded and ran away south.

When you woke up, you were able to pack up camp and make some breakfast, discussing of your findings between shifts.
