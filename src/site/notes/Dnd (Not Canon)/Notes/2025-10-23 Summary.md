After you wake up, you go to the park and have flying shenanigans. You checked out a statue:

>There stands a large figure, their hands lifted to the sky, eyes looking down to the observer. A wheel of thorns rotates around them. Two smaller statues are also seen. The middle is looking down to the mote that surrounds them, tears hanging from his eyes. The smallest is a figure with its hands clasped together. It has a third eye on its forehead, forever closed. There is a sign talking about it. The name of the commissioner is carved out and illegible.

You went on a tour at the Braveheart Magica Factory, the heart of the Spark of Innovation. At the very end, you were able to mend an old draft map of the Spark, revealing the Architect's name as Elouise Braveheart. The tour guide then removed the piece you've mended, and tells you how the city doesn't talk about her due to her abandoning the city and letting the magic run dry.
