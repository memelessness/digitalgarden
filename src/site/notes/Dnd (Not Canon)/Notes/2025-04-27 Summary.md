After slaying a great beast in the small town of Châtelaine (fantasy Louisianna), the party has found themselves taking a break from adventuring in hopes of gaining fortune through the local casino. 

However, your big break never came. And while discussing your poor decisions (in pursuit of even worse decisions), you decided to take a job from the job board. MISSING: Warlock Patron.

Traveling up the river, you came across an old fishing shack of Madryck Roslof. While not quite sound of mind, he talks about his Patron: Guinevere, a High Faerie that he recalls living in a desert.

He told you to travel north, and after a very brief encounter with magical phenomena, you find yourself at your next destination. The Festival of Ember and Feathers.
