You traveled with Moxxie and Tenor to the Spark of Innovation, a city located in the center of the Flats. The flats have a noticeable lack of magic in the air compared to the little experience you've had in the Faerielands. Moxxie tells you that it doesn't know the story, but the Spark was built off of Magitech back in the day (after the Fracture) and was rerouted to the land when the magic ran out.

You make it to Moxxie's apartment, the living area well kept and sparsely decorated. Its workshop, however, is a bit of a mess with papers strewn about. As its cleaning, you notice there are a few items around the workshop. Some have fuel cells that are smaller but not dissimilar to those of the hyenas. These cells, however, are empty.

When you ask Moxxie about the cells, it mentions how a lot of people in the Flats don't really practice in Magitech anymore, due to the nature of the city's founding. 

You found an enchanted spyglass that shows the user what people are at their bare essentials. You used it on Moxxie:

>Instead of seeing the person before you, you see a more slender figure. Its slightly taller, feathers longer and less curled around the edges. Its eyes are more akin to pinpricks, their definition more hazy. Its color too is more gray, aside from yellow rings around the eyes. Finally, you notice an aura around it. Not quite evil, but still not quite good. It is a selfish kind of hunger that burrows beneath the skin. It looks back to you, glaring, and you notice a wheel of thorns around its neck.

Moxxie takes the spyglass from you, telling you to "Forget about her. She's dead."

You also look through some notebooks

>The notes are still accurate, as far as you can tell, though they seem to be more theoretical, jumping between dimensions from one problem to the next with little to even show such a conversion. It seems like the math is tracking something magical, with some abandoned attempts to convert to electricity and vice-versa. Truthfully, you know there’s no such conversion.
>
>And at the end of the journal, there are only scribbles left. There are some notes in the margins. Some akin to the ramblings of confronted with their own religious-induced psychosis.

Another batch of notes talks about taking magic from a "higher power," not really specifying much more after but you have seen Moxxie take magic from items before.
