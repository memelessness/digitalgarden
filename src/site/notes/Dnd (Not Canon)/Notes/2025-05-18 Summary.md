You’ve made your way to the Festival of Ember and Feathers! Upon arrival, you each purchased a ticket. Some with money, others with barters. You were also given a magical map of the circus.

From there, you have played an assortment of games such as a rhyme battle with a Kenku and a Fairy Cake eating contest, of which you all were very mighty and determined.

One of you have also learned (or at least believe you do) of the importance of keeping hold of your Ticket.
