After two of you refreshed your Ticket, picking up fae curses along the way, your party finds yourselves at the Ice Rink of Illusions. While in line, you came across a halfling couple. As one was proposing, the other began to have a fit of uncontrollable laughter caused by a nearby magical item. 

In entering the icerink, you observed some of the statues, reminiscing on memories and sometimes even taking the shape you had in those memories.

After your time in reminiscence, you found Ruben, the distraught halfling, on his hands and knees, speaking quietly into something in the ice. He was soon pulled under, leaving behind a ring box and his ticket.

In your time of fucking around and finding out, you did find an Old Trumpet Mouthpiece underneath the ice, which had the aura of a magic from an unknown realm. (Shadowlands)
