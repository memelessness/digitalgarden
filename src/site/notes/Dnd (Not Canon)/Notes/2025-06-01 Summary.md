On your way to the next attraction, you are visited by Redd the squirrel and a tree ent that are gifting guests of the festival with magical dandelions. That let you make a wish.

You then make your way to the Carousel where you see a vision of two High Faerie women traveling by carriage in a forest.

As you continued to make your way through the carnival, your party picked up another person with the same goal! You then continued to play some carnival games!

In your wanderings, you met the ringleader of the festival, Lucien Yarrow, who has a penchant for flashier magics. In talking to him, you took notice of his attitude when he found a lone ticket, passing it to a staff member who hurriedly returned it to the rightful owner.
