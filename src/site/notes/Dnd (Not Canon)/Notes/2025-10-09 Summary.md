After walking for some time, you finally made it to the beach where you meet Moxxie (a bird-like fae, it/its) and Tenor (a fire genasi, he/him) enjoying some music at their truck.

Upon mentioning the hyena you'd just fought, Moxxie is quick to return to the scene, frustrated that you destroyed the core of the automata.

You are soon ambushed by the remaining creatures, and as you fight them, you are able to recover 3 cores. One of the creatures ambushed Moxxie, and your party gets to see one of the greatest displays of magic.

>A crackling energy soon erupts out, distorted light spilling from within the fuel cell of the automata. It starts as a flickering glimmer, yet soon enough it grows bigger, and hotter, until the weight of it all becomes too much. Its inevitable collapse is radiant, and all devouring. All life, all matter, all light consumed in its wake.

When Moxxie is finally able to get up, you see it pulling magic from the intact core and absorbing it. Moxxie also takes the magic snake from the first hyena for study. You then all leave to return to the beach.

Upon returning to the truck, Skink took an ominous call. You then all got into the back of the truck and make your way to the Flats.
