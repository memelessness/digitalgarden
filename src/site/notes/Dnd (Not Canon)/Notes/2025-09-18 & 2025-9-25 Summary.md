You're given a task to help get some "Rats" off their land, as they've been camping on the beaches south for a few days now, when they usually only pass through.

Kit meets up with you while you eat. You discover that she and her mother went back to the Gods' Tree (The one you encountered with the eyes and the shrine). She is very surprised and thankful that you fixed the carvings. You learn that the carvings were made by her mother, and Kit is learning how to make the same figurines.

It got a bodyguard since then but hasn't raided the village since. She said the "bird guy" won't be too difficult to negotiate with since even she could take it in a fight. The bodyguard, on the other hand, is "like... 10 ft tall!!" and would definitely be scary if it came down to it. 

After leaving the village, you soon encountered a lone mechanical hyena (did you know a pack of hyenas is called a "cackle?"), which was moving in an erratic manner. In fighting it, you were able to see it's fuel cell that was kept in its rib cage "**It swirls with something mysterious. It looks like a snake, a thick black sludge rolling off of it, peppering the light of the magic.**" After not too long fighting (like 10 second game time), you were able to explode it. The fuel cell burst open dramatically and the magic spilled onto the ground, recollected by the land.
