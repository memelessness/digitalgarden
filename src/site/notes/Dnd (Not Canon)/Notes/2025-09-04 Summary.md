You set back out on your adventure, following the tracks that were left in the morning. The tracks from that morning were still fresh, growing smaller and smaller down the path until they were gone. You were able to find small claw marks in the trees that soon disappeared with the scent of Jasmine.

After some time, you came upon a great tree. Instead of faces, it was covered in eyes. Along the branches, there are orbs of light called "Kitsunebi" or "Fox Fire" dancing among the branches. In the hollow of the tree was a statue sat between a gilded box and a bunch of old fox-shaped figurines. Investigating the tree, you took notice of 3 eyes in an ordered pyramid shape. 

"At the top, their eye is open, the shape made up of a wheel of thorns

To the left, his eye is open, a tear clinging to the bottom

To the right, it's eye is closed. "

You noticed after one of you climbed the tree that the top of this pyramid was watching you up until you climbed down.

You cast mending on the figurines, bringing them to their former glory. You lit the incense in the box. Its smell was overpoweringly of smoke, but there was a gentle scent of Watercress Lilies. The statue opens its eye, a tear falling from it as it points south, beckoning you to follow.

After some time, the footsteps returned in sets. You overheard bickering about the Fox staying up all night to watch you.

You meet:

Kit (She/Her): A Kitsune girl who's very excited for adventure
Strings (They/Them): A racoon teen who loves to play music. They are very *very* warry of you
Thai (He/Him): The antlered teen and the mom of the group

After giving them a honeyed snack (good) and firing off your gun (very very bad), they brought you to yet another tree. Kit climbed up the tree, pulling down a ladder.
