After watching the show at the Big Top, you decide to go to Story Time where Eowynn was telling the children a story.

https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ccrhEPpDy60CdGQQ7kUusCoFDg8qP5Fn2rKdlZqiba0/edit?usp=sharing

After the story, you spoke to Eowynn about some happenings in the circus. After a long time of prodding in the real world, you were able to say the right words that allowed him to talk to you without breaking his pact to the Circus.

He told you the rhyme to get into the faerielands. Soon after you returned to the Ice Rink of Illusions where you ran into Viktor Yarrow and Lucien Yarrow and you fought. After demoralizing a gay man and his disabled husband, you were able to say the rhyme, open the portal, and make your way to the Faerielands
