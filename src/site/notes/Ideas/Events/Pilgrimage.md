**The Primage** is the mass exodus of residents in [[Places/Territories/Coast/Ocean\|Ocean]] villages to the mainland of the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Faerielands\|Faerielands]] due to increasingly inhospitable conditions resulting in a greater need for involving in trade.


[[People/Pirates/Marlow\|Marlow]] notably moved to the [[Places/Territories/Grasslands\|Grasslands]] with his parents as a young child due to whalefall upon their village.
