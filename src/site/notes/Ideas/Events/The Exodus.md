**The Exodus** is an event that happened in the [[Places/Territories/Red Woods\|Red Woods]] where upon Highlord [[People/Highlords/Umber\|Umber]]'s coronation, many [[Ideas/Concepts/High Faerie\|High Faerie]] folk left their home for another [[Places/Territories/Territories\|Territories]]. Many left for the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Coast\|Coast]], which better followed their values on race, others went to the [[Places/Territories/Oasis/Oasis\|Oasis]] due to their own prosperity.
