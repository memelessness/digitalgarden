**Unraveling** is what happens when a faerie calls upon more [[Ideas/Concepts/Magic\|Magic]] than their body can readily handle.  The body begins to crack, revealing the magic underneath

Known Characters to have experienced Unraveling:
[[People/NYX STORY RELEVENT/Nyx Snow\|Nyx Snow]]- Nyx is the only character to have survived unraveling twice.  The first was after the death of [[People/NYX STORY RELEVENT/Lily\|Lily]], leading to the tear marks on her cheeks. The second was with Mycan.

[[People/NYX STORY RELEVENT/Mycan\|Mycan]] - Mycan called upon the magic of the [[Places/Territories/Mountains\|Mountains]] to create the storm that still surrounds it today. This instance left deep scars on his arms.  

[[People/Changelings (There's a lot I'm so sorry)/Moxxie\|Moxxie]]- Moxxie used to have a partnership with the [[People/Gods/Sun\|Sun]] that lead to it unraveling, leading to the sunburst burnt into its chest.
