**The God Tree** is a massive tree in the [[Places/Territories/Red Woods\|Red Woods]] where instead of faces, it is covered in a multitude of eyes that belong to the [[Ideas/Concepts/Old Gods\|Old Gods]].

In the hollow of the tree is a shrine made to the [[People/Gods/Moon\|Moon]]. It has aged overtime, left with a box of incense. Behind it are small fox-shaped figurines carved from wood. Many of them are rotting. There are two that seem fairly new. One is a very well detailed fox carved from a light colored wood and silver spirals painted around it. Another is a rock with its face painted, laying in a bed of leaves.

The three biggest eyes form the shape of a pyramid:

At the top, the [[People/Gods/Sun\|Sun]]. Their eye is open, the shape made up of a wheel of thorns

To the left, the [[People/Gods/Moon\|Moon]]. His eye is open, a tear clinging to the bottom

To the right, [[People/Gods/The Well\|The Well]]. It's eye is closed. This is not because The Well is dead, but rather its [[Ideas/Concepts/Magic\|Magic]] cannot reach the tree. Aka the Mother Above
