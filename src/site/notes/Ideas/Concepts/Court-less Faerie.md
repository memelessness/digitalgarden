**Court-less Faeries** are Faeries that either not born to a court or were exiled during the time of the [[Places/Seasonal Courts/Seasonal Courts\|Seasonal Courts]]. Many would find themselves living in the [[Places/Seasonal Courts/Court of Stars\|Court of Stars]], which was a court only in name but aided the court-less in acquiring [[Ideas/Concepts/Magic\|Magic]].

After [[Ideas/Events/The Fracture\|The Fracture]], the likelihood of being court-less became a more rare phenomena, as the dynamics between [[Places/Territories/Territories\|Territories]] has changed drastically. If someone is exiled from their original court, they can ally with another.
