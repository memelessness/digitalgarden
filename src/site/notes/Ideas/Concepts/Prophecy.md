**Prophecies** are placed upon all beings, but are widely celebrated and acknowledged by the residents of the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Faerielands\|Faerielands]]. 

[[People/Gods/The Well\|The Well]] in the [[Places/Territories/Forest\|Forest]] can be sought to hear ones prophecy.
