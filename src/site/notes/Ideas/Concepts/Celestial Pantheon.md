**The Celestial Pantheon** is a group of [[Ideas/Concepts/Old Gods\|Old Gods]] that can be seen in the sky

The [[People/Gods/Sun\|Sun]], the [[People/Gods/Moon\|Moon]], and [[People/Gods/The Well\|The Well]] were seated at the Thrones of the pantheon.

The Well, formerly known as Mother Above, used to sit at the Crown of the Thrones, positioned at the top of the three with the Moon to the left and the Sun to the right.

When the Mother Above disappeared, its true name fading from time all together, the [[People/Gods/Sun\|Sun]] took over as the Crown of the Celestial Pantheon, rewriting history for their benefit.
