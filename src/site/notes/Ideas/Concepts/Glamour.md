**Glamours** are enchanted items that disguise oneself and take on another visage.


Glamours enchanted by a [[Ideas/Races/Changeling\|Changeling]] is the most valuable, as the magic has no scent and the disguise is nearly indistinguishable. 


[[Ideas/Concepts/Lesser Faerie\|Lesser Faerie]] folk of the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Coast\|Coast]] will often purchase Glamours to better conform into society, though the need for it as decreased since the coronation of [[People/Pirates/Mary\|Mary]].
