**Changelings** are a shapeshifting race that can belong to the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Mortal Realm\|Mortal Realm]] or [[Places/Planes of Existance/Faerielands\|Faerielands]]. A notable quality of changelings is that their magic is odorless and they cannot accidentally attune to magical items. 

The form of changelings take after different parasitic creatures, such as various fungus, cuckoo birds, wasps, etc.

Changelings raised in the Mortal Realm often do not know of their heritage. They have a lifespan of a few hundred years if they are not exposed to magic. Changelings of the Mortal Realm may be able to readapt to the Faerielands after an adequate exposure, as they would not be aligned with any territory.

Changelings were blessed by [[People/Gods/The Well\|The Well]], formerly known as the Mother Above. It created them to find loopholes in their making, staying hidden with their unscented magic, able to lie through the guise of personas. 

Many changelings find their way to [[Places/Territories/Flats/The Spark of Innovation\|The Spark of Innovation]], many old families like the Bravehearts residing there.
