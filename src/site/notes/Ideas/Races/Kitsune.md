**Kitsune** are fox-like [[Ideas/Concepts/Lesser Faerie\|Lesser Faerie]]s that were favored by the [[People/Gods/Sun\|Sun]] and the [[People/Gods/Moon\|Moon]].

Every generation, two babies will be blessed Gold for the Sun and Silver for the Moon. The Sun is apathetic to the creatures on the ground, sharing their blessing but never intervening. The Moon is very caring, being watchful over those he blesses.

[[People/Terra\|Terra]] was blessed by the Moon as a baby, and he has protected her ever since, even going so far as to step in when hunters attacked her as a young child. He, however, was not able to protect her from the heartbreak of having her child ripped from her.




[[People/Terra\|Terra]] is the last full-blooded kitsune from her clan. She shares her traditions with her children,    [[People/Pirates/Felix\|Felix]], who was poached by the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Coastal Underground Fight Ring\|Coastal Underground Fight Ring]] at a young age, and [[People/Kit\|Kit]].
