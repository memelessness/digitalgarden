Characters: [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Cosmos\|Cosmos]], [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Aidoneus\|Aidoneus]]

Description: The one where Cosmos catches Aidoneus smoking in the garden outside the greenhouse, lamenting about his relationship with Moss

**

"... Moss thinks xe loves me." Aidoneus muttered under his breath, bringing the joint to his mouth, taking in another hit if only to busy his mind.

Cosmos felt a sort of pull in her chest as she gingerly sat beside him, reaching her hand out. Aidoneus obliged as he passed the smoke around, and she wrapped her lips around it with a sort of delicate grace.

"Moss is usually pretty sure of xemself." She spoke softly, ribbons of smoke spilling from her mouth. It tasted... soft. Sweeter than usual. She'd wondered for a moment if he'd just bought a new strain or he'd taken whatever she'd just been leaving on the drying rack. "Why are you so worried. I've seen how you look at xem." 

Aidoneus relaxed more into the bench, raking his fingers through his hair, "That's... beside the point. I can't... I don't..." He was already lost for words. Cosmos wondered just how long he'd been out here. 

Time passed in the silence as he gathered himself, the void between them filled with that of crickets singing and the water of a nearby fountain churning. The night was beautiful. Most nights were beautiful. Cosmos couldn't recall a single ugly night since she'd come to live here. It all just felt like... some sort of fantasy. The sort of thing you tell children to make them believe goodness can persevere through all the hardships... The kind of stories that buried any sort of pain away, until you were forced to confront it again.
  
"I'm... not allowed to fall in love." He finally settled on, sliding more down the bench. He looked up to Cosmos to find her face contorting to that excuse, "I don’t know… It’s some dumb prophecy nonsense I just…. I don’t know. I don’t want to tell xem that but I also don’t think I should lie either.”  
  
Cosmos sat still and listened to her friend, watching how the flowerbushes would sway with the breeze, “Moss is the kind of person who really loves with xir entirety. I think any way of letting xem down would hurt.”  
  
“Yeah. Like you’d know from experience.” His tone was sharp. Something that cut so deep that Cosmos could feel it in her very core.

She took another drag before offering the joint back and she released a slow exhale, letting the herb wash a calmness over her as she tried to find her words again, “That’s not fair, Aid. You know it’s complicated.

Aidoneus let out a sort of grunt, swatting her hand away, “I don’t need to hear this again just… Just shut up, Cos.” An instant look of guilt had crossed his face as the words left his mouth, and he turned to look away.

Cosmos decided not to dig deeper into it. She knew she’d hurt him enough times. It was only fair. So instead, she gripped at the fabric in her skirt, loosing a shaky breath.

“... I’m sorry.. I didn’t mean to-”

“Don’t be.” Her voice was cold for a moment, before she could remind herself of the softness this situation needed, “You know I…” Her tone felt calmer, only the slightest waver was she spoke, “I’d often wonder if coming to the Mountains was a mistake after…” She made a vague motion with her hand, “After… well… everything.”

Aidoneus looked back to her, pushing himself up to sit a bit straighter, “Cos, that’s not you’re fault.”  
  
“I know it’s just…” She brought the joint back to her mouth, taking in a deep inhale before snuffing the end of it out on the stone of the bench, “It wasn’t fair of me to have left you like that… without any sign. Any letter.” She glanced over to see wide eyes just watching her. An odd mixture of compassion and calculation. She looked away, wiping her eyes, “I did write letters, you know. Not that it means much now.”

Aidoneus turned to look away when he noticed it was making Cosmos uncomfortable, placing a hand over hers as she fumbled against her words. There was a calmness between them, as all the stresses that had been piling up had begun to tear at the weave of her strong persona and bring her almost back to who she used to be.

“I just feel like I played such a huge roll in hurting you and I… I just miss my best friend.” She managed to croak out as the tears began to spill.

“You didn’t.” Aidoneus spoke firmly, “Cos I… Dammit I’m not in the right mindset to be dealing with this.” He pinched his brow with his freehand, a sort of irritation heavy in his tone.

Cosmos took in a shuddering breath, pulling her hand away. She watched him for a moment as he frustratingly tried to gather himself. For all she knew, he could’ve been lying just to save her feelings, and that brought a chill to run through her blood, “I think you should talk to Moss.” She stood up, finding a flower pot to drop the remains of the joint in, “I’ve seen how you look at each other. You’d make a great couple, you know.”

Cosmos took a few steps past Aidoneus, continuing on her walk.
