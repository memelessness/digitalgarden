Characters:[[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Moss\|Moss]], [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Artemis\|Artemis]]

Description: The one where [[Ideas/Events/Spirit Nights\|Spirit Nights]] are introduced and Moss laments about xir parents.

**

The spirit night happens maybe a month after Artemis arrives and she's drawn to the violin playing from faraway and then sees spirits *everywhere.* She quietly walks up to Moss as xe continued to play, watching as xe seemed to be focused on a specific pair.


"They're my parents." Moss spoke softly over the music, watching the two women hold each other close as they swayed to the song, "There was a skirmish 40 years back. One of our allies called for our help, and I stayed behind." Xe swallowed hard, the notes wobbling just a bit.

"... What happened?" The Hunter inquired, maintaining the gentle tone xe had.

The music stopped, the two spirits looking up confused, "They took the majority of our guard with them... and they were ambushed. Most of them died, only a few soldiers were able to retreat... but my mothers would rather die fighting."

"... Have you tried talking to them? They're right there."

"... They're not of this world anymore. There's no way to talk to them."

The Hunter shifted in their seat, watching as the two pulled apart, still holding hands, "They can hear the music, though."

"Because music's not really of this world either. It's not something simply bound to one plane." Xe lifted up the violin to play another song. The smaller of the two brightened up, jumping with glee as she talked to the other in noiseless words. Maybe it was her favorite song, "It's my only way to talk to them."
