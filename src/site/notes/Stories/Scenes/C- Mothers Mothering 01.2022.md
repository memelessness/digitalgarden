Description: The one where Moss is sad about not asking Cosmos out

**

Moss’ mothers entered the room, softly approaching xir bed.  
  
“Had we misunderstood?” The smaller of the two spoke quietly. She’d done most of the preparations for the day. Prepared a nice picnic just by the river, shooed all the guards and staff away so that Moss and Cosmos could have their space. She was so confident the two loved each other… and yet here was her spawn, sobbing pathetically into a collection of pillows and furs.

“She’s promised to another.” Moss spoke weakly into xir pillow, “Her prophecy said so… She told me before I could even ask.” Xe couldn’t help but clench xir fists, “She’s promised to a damn human, and there’s nothing I could do about it.” And the tears began to pour faster, “I… I couldn’t do that to her… What kind of friend would I be if I…” 

“... Well that’s a rubbish excuse.”

“Gwen.” The Lord of the Mountain warned, placing a firm hand on her wife’s shoulder.  
  
“What? Has she seen xem?” She motioned toward her wounded son, “Xe’s got your eyes and my, admittedly, dashing good looks. What girl wouldn’t want xem?”  
  
“Love of my life and stars of my nights, would you kindly please shut the fuck up.” She sighed, seating herself next to Moss, “I’m sorry, Little Fawn. You know how your mother loves to play matchmaker.” She reached over to xem, sweeping some hair from xir eyes, “I should’ve considered maybe she’d have a love prophecy.”

Xe managed to grunt a response, curling more into the blankets.

The two exchanged a look, Guinevere reaching to dote on her child before calmly being declined by the other.  
  
“It’s okay to let this break you.” She spoke with stern compassion, “We can wait to talk, if you need space.”

Moss rolled to xir other side, curling in on xemself, “.... I’ll come back stronger, I just need some time.”

She motioned her wife out the door before looking back, “I don’t care whether you come back stronger, just come back you, alright?”

There was silence before all xe was able to manage was a weak hum of acknowledgement.

“... Alright.” She stepped outside the threshold of the room, her own heart breaking quietly at xir sorrow, “Whenever you’re ready to come out, I’ll make sure the staff has food ready.” And she quietly shut the door, allowing xem to rest quietly in the dark… Allowing xem time to heal.
