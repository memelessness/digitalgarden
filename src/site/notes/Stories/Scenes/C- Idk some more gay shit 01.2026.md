Cold fingers trial up Moss' chest, sending a shudder down xir spine. 



"You have your reservations." Aidoneus spoke in that resolute tone that had a way of feeling comforting to Moss, seeping into xir skin and making a home underneath.

"I think it's a bit late for reservations, all things considered." Xe spoke with a bit of a laugh, leaning in closer to continue where they'd left off.

But Aidoneus pulled back, reaching up to lace his fingers behind xir neck, "Talk to me first. You seem stressed."

"I've been busy."

Aidoneus hummed a soft note, as if considering xir words, "You've been *stupid*... with all due respect. You're able to *not* be busy, though.” He trailed his fingers along the back of xir neck, feeling how it bristled.

“Since when do *you* want to talk?” Moss felt the words simply fall from xir mouth, regretting the bitterness that had woven into them, “I’m… sorry I just…”

“No no, you’re right.” Aidoneus pulled back a bit, his usual mischievous little grin coming a bit more forced, “We don’t talk. Not really. Not like… like *couples* are supposed to.” He hesitated a bit, looking away, “I just… thought maybe you *needed* to talk for a bit. Though I suppose it’s not very fun, either.”

Moss sat up, pulling xir shirt back together as their eyes met once again, “I could do without the insults, for starters.” Xe sighed, moving over to lay down beside Aidoneus. Xe reached out to hold his hand firmly, as if to remind him that they were ok, “I’m sorry I’m not really good at… talking.”

“Neither am I.” Aidoneus spoke softer than Moss had ever thought possible, rolling over to face xem better.

Moss let go of his hand, reaching up to cup his face and gliding a careful thumb across his cheek, “It might be good for us.” Xe reached over to his hair, playing with a curl between xir fingers. It was a welcome distraction.

Aidoneus leaned in to press his forehead against Moss’. It was a form of sentiment xe had shared with him time and time again. An affection only the Mountainfolk had ever shared with those deeply important to them. Though this was the first time Aidoneus had even dared initiate.

“I’ve just been worried about you, lately.” He started to speak, closing his eyes, “You’ve been working yourself way too hard lately. And I’m just left watching you destroy yourself in the process and it’s just all too…” He trailed off a bit, cracking his eyes open to meet xir gaze, “And, *selfishly* I want you all to myself, and I can’t have that if you’re overworked.”

It was jarring, watching him switch from genuine to jokester in such a swift turn. Moss wanted to point that out, but xe felt like that’d be taking more as a deflection than genuine concern, “I can talk to my uncle, next time they get back.”

“You need to work things out with Cosmos.” The words left a sort of sting in the air, silencing everything else, “Listen, I don’t know what’s going on between you too, and truthfully, I wouldn’t be surprised if it *was* her fault.” He pressed a finger to Moss’ chest, “And that’s *not* because of the what we have going on. I just know she’s a stubborn mule on a good day.”

Moss couldn’t help but chuckle to that. A sort of dry laugh that would only bubble in times of discomfort, “I can handle the work just fine.”

“Babes, you are drowning. And it disturbing that you don’t even realize it.” Aidoneus was firm in his words, “We *barely* spend time with each other, and I can tell you’re not getting any sleep, and I really do believe the only breaks you are allowing yourself are when I’m able to pull you away from work and that’s not healthy.”

Moss simply nodded, unable to find the right words to get xem out of this conversation. So there was nothing really left to do but take it. To really listen to what xir parter had to offer and allow it to fully sink in.

“If anything,” He reached his hands up, holding xir face between them, “You need to at least split the work. With Cos. I’m not helping you with paperwork or whatever it is you’re doing that sounds terrible.” He doubled down, trying to decipher whatever Moss was thinking.

And Moss watched him for a moment, *really* taking in the time to look at him. It really sat with xem how someone so brazen could carry so much care and devotion in their tone while still trying to hold onto such a delicate facade of lackadaisical whimsy.

Xe lifted a hand to cover his, turning xir face to place a kiss in his palm before turning back to face him, “I’ll talk to her tomorrow.” Aidoneus raised an inquisitive eyebrow, loosing a more genuine laugh from xem, “I will! I promise! I wouldn’t lie.”

Aidoneus shrugged, a grin tugging at the end of his lips, “Could be fun.”

“I think not.”
