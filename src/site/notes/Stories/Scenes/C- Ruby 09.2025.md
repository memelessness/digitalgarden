Description: The one where Nyx has a friend (who dies not too long after this scene) This was also inspired by a very specific Spamtenna fic

**

Nexus held onto the lantern, the ambient warmth enough to ward off the barbaric chill of the Winter Court. They had just left the threshold of the capitol, walking side by side as the snow crunched beneath their feet. It hadn’t iced yet. Nexus found comfort in this. It meant they could relax into their steps, not needing to keep their guard, if only for a moment. 

They looked to their company as she looked into the distance, eyes wandering against something unspoken. Ruby was beautiful in Nexus’ opinion. She was high fae, pale skin and tight coils of ginger tucked back into the hood of her coat. The light of the lantern reflecting against her eyes. There was a spark there, far more admirable than Nexus could muster anymore. They could see their old self sometimes in her, and there was an odd comfort to that.

“What are we?” Ruby was the first to break the silence, her hand tucked into a heavy coat.

Nexus stopped walking, turning to face their company as she continued for a few more steps, “... What?”

“I mean.” Her shoulders raised, not quite turning around. Instead she was focused on the falling snow, “What is…” She pulled her hands from her coat, motioning vaguely into the air, “This. Us. Are we just work associates? Friends? More?”                 

“More?” Nexus felt themselves shrink at that, drowning in their own coat as they watched their feet in sudden curiosity, “What do you mean more?”

“We are going on a stroll in the snow, isolated from prying eyes, just to ‘hang out.’ Nex, that’s one of those cliches you see in a play.” Her voice softened, hands gripping tightly at her side, “And other than that, there’s been glances. Little exchanges. A sort of… comfort between us? I don’t know. It could just be me? Is… Is it just me?”

Nexus’ stomach dropped at the very thought. To be… more? They’d never felt something like that before; never had a proper reference of such a thing. There was no way their… parents were more. They’d asked their grandmother before only to get some vague response about duty and community. So how were they supposed to know if that’s what they were feeling?

But Ruby was so kind to her! So warm! So… So considerate. And genuine. Nexus enjoyed her company, for sure, but was that enough?

“Ruby I… um… You…” Nexus hesitated, trying desperately to say something. That maybe just letting the words fall out would help them sort this all out. But nothing would loose itself from their tongue.

Her shoulders dropped a bit, head cocking to the side. Tight curls fell out of the hood of her coat, framing her face delicately, “You can say no. I can handle it… It wouldn’t change anything.” She added the last part assuredly, to leave that promise in the air.

“But… I don’t know what to say.”
 
“Then react!” Ruby was becoming more frustrated, waving hands through the falling snow as she stepped closer, “Walk off! Get closer! Be disgusted! Or excited! Push me away! Hug me! I don’t know! Just-” She finally locked eyes with Nexus, her emotions betraying her as she paused to catch her breath, plumes of steam rising from her mouth, “Just… Don’t just stare at me like that.”

Nexus wasn’t comfortable with this sort of thing. Not only in figuring out their own emotions… But they’d also been warned about this. About how attachments could be held against them. Who were they to get someone else involved, “You don’t understand.”

“I want to though.” Ruby stood her ground, pale skin flushed red as the cold burned into her face.

Ruby reached into Nexus’ coat, a careful hand cradling them by the waist. They flinched on contact, bringing Ruby to pull away. Yet a sort of softness flashed in her eyes, reaching a hand upward to move a strand of hair behind their ears. She leaned in, smoothing Nexus’ coat against their body before letting it settle in the same place.

The layers had dulled the sensation, a pain that’d felt so normal to their body rushing against their skin before settling into something safe. Something they could maybe find comfort in, “That’s… um…”

“Bold? Audacious? Ruthless?”

Nexus looked up to her, their mouth aimlessly fumbling against a bunch of different words before finally being able to let out, “... Intrepid.”

“Intrepid.” Ruby hummed, pulling Nexus just a little closer, “Ok. Now we’re getting somewhere.”

Nexus’ shoulders went slack, the lantern dangling at the edges of their fingers. They simply just watched quietly, bringing their freehand up to clutch the sleeve of Ruby’s coat.

“I’m leaving for my next assignment in the morning.” Ruby spoke breathlessly, pulling away just to take Nexus’ hand into her own, “Just think about it, alright?”

Nexus took another breath in, gloved fingers lacing amidst each other, “Ok.” Their voice was barely louder than a whisper, making no effort to pull away. And like that, they continued their walk until they’d reached the city once more.
