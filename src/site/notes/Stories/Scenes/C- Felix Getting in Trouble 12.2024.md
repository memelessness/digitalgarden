Characters:[[People/Pirates/Felix\|Felix]], [[People/Pirates/Mary\|Mary]]

Description: Felix tries to arson the fight ring and is brought before the coastal lord

**

Felix was in the middle of being escorted to the Coastal Lord’s hearing room.

Today was… eventful to say the least. After causing quite a ruckus, he’d spent some time behind bars. Now, he was usually pretty experienced in this sort of thing. Most cops were negligent at best, and his magic was extremely useful at making an escape. He didn’t, however, consider just how much damage he’d done. Because of this, extra protocols were put into place.

Very soon after getting captured, the officers had slapped on some enchanted manacles around his wrists. They were uncomfortable to say the least, and prevented him from blinking away. It felt weird, being so disconnected from his magic… yet it wasn’t unfamiliar either. He wondered, for a moment, if the cops were in on the fighting rings that hide in the bones of the city.

He was thrown to the ground, forced to kneel as he struggled to pull himself up.

“Your Majesty.” One of the guards stepped forward, “We bring before you the man who attacked the streets of this fair kingdom.”

Really laying it on thick, Felix thought, trying to move so his chest was off the floor. He wanted to look this old man in the eye as they sentenced him… But this was far more difficult with his hands to his back.

“We have found no records of citizenship, and nothing on their person to indicate their territory allegiance. They have been charged with arson and the endangerment of residents to the city.”

Felix couldn’t help but laugh, finally able to lift his head, “Your majesty,” He spoke with sickly venom on his tongue, “I’ll have you know that the-” 

He stopped himself, looking at the man that sat at the throne. Now, it’d been a while since he’d been to [City Name], but he knew about the stubborn Highlord. A man who’d turn a blind eye to the happenings of his city, so long as it didn’t harm his good name. A man that seemed he would never willingly step down from his position of control. Yet who sat in the throne, with cool and calculated precision, was a welcomed surprise.

“... Uncle Mary?” He was confused. Yes, this was Mary. He could tell just by the smell of their magic.. And yet… something was off.

Mary sat up as they made eye contact with Felix, their breath hitching for just a moment. They raised their hand, dismissing the guards, “And what is it you’ll have me know?” They stood as the guards made their exit, and slowly walked to him.

Felix didn’t really know what to expect. On one hand, he was pretty sure he was going to get out of this easy. Mary had always been soft, especially for his crew… but now, something was off. It was like something in him had died, chipping away at him as the weight of his responsibilities piled on top of his shoulders. For a moment, Felix even wondered if he’d make it out of this alive.
“Where’s Marlow?” He spoke up, watching as Mary stopped in their approach. Felix hesitated for a moment, moving his arms against the manacles. He could see the hurt in their eyes, despite their otherwise cold demeanor. 

“He’s…” Mary started, letting out a sigh as they shed their facade, waving a hand to release Felix from his bounds, “What’re you doing here, Felix. You could’ve gotten in real trouble.”

Felix let out a great sigh of relief, massaging the tenderness in his wrists. Despite everything, it was still Mary, “I was… paying someone a visit.” He danced around the truth, not really sure if he was supposed to address them as a Lord or a friend. Even so, he knew there was only so much Mary could do. No Highlord has ever had success in establishing drastic change, and knowing Mary, he knew they’d probably find it better to stay out of it.

“Arson, Felix.” They spoke with the seriousness of an exhausted father, “People don’t just commit arson.”

Felix had given him a very pointed look, only to be met by a very flustered man needing to back themself out of a corner. He’d remembered seeing Mary in their prime. Arson seemed to be the least of their worries.

“That was-” Felix couldn’t meet Mary in the eye anymore, deciding to instead look around the room, “That was where I ran from… Before sneaking onto the ship.” He felt uncomfortable, not wanting to think too much on those days. All he’d wanted was some sort of vindication… but was this really worth it? By now, Milo was probably worried about where he is.

And then Felix could feel it. The magic that had stretched from the ground beneath him, pooling to the same point in the room. Right around Mary. He could even see it; something far more powerful than what he’d seen from them before. 

“That was here.” Mary spoke in a low tone, dripping with ire. Felix wondered if he’d ever seen them angry before… Though… he knew they were a father now. Maybe imagining their child being taken from their home only to spend the rest of their life imprisoned… He wondered if his mother would’ve put up this much of a fight.

Felix nodded, and the magic dissipated. Remnants still lingered, circling around them.

Mary nodded, walking past them, “I’ll take care of it. I’ll send someone to take you to a doctor.”

“Mary, I-”

But Mary just continued walking.

Felix couldn’t help himself, using his magic to blink in front of Mary as he stretched his arms out, “What is going on with you?” He tried to make himself bigger, only to be met with eyes of cold steel, “You seem off man! You’ve barely even talked to me and now you’re going to… What? Talk to them? Ask them kindly to stop? Mary, where is Marlow?”

Mary placed a hand on Felix’s shoulder, that chilling mask slipping back into place almost seamlessly, “Who said anything about talking.” It wasn’t a question, but a promise as he pushed past Felix once again.

Felix watch Mary as he walked away, unable to stir himself to call out to them.
