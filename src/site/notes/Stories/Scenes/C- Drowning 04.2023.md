Characters:[[People/Pirates/Mary\|Mary]], [[People/Pirates/Marlow\|Marlow]]

Description: Mary falls overboard and is saved

**

It started with a crash. The air had been knocked out of Mary’s lungs the mere moment he’d come into contact with the water. In his dazed state, he managed to open his eyes. The shimmering expanse of light surrounded him, slowly becoming darkness as he descended further. 

He’d spend but a moment thinking to himself how pathetic this was. How could he let himself fall to such an unfitting end? The crowned prince of a territory surrounded by beaches falling into the ocean’s very clutch… And he didn’t even know how to swim. What even would be the point in trying.

Another crash was above him as he was just getting ready to close his eyes, barreling toward him with a stream of bubbles trailing behind. He weakly reached his hand up as the water continued to weigh him down. He remembered the ray of light, a dark figure reaching back for him. He’d think for a moment just how beautiful they were, their hair stretching out as soon as their momentum slowed. And then it was nothing.

“Easy now.” A voice tried to sooth Mary  as he heaved on the saltwater that’d swam around in his lungs, “You’re okay. Just relax. The water will leave on its own.”

Mary had rolled over, holding himself up with shaking arms. He felt a thump against his back, dislodging whatever remained. He found himself heaving, gasping for fresh air.

“We’ve all fallen overboard once or twice.” Milo started, laughing under his breath, “You’re lucky Marlow was watching you. Otherwise none of us would’ve-” He was interrupted by a jab to his side, and he muttered his protests.

Marlow had reached over to roll Mary back around, propping him up against the wall, “You’re alright now. Just focus on catching your breath, okay?” 

Mary held his chest for a moment, looking up to Marlow who’d been just as drenched as he was, “I slipped… and…”

“I know.” Marlow laughed, sweeping the hair that’d been stuck to Mary’s face, “I guess you could consider it initiation. The sea likes to choose inopportune times to pull you under.” 

“... I don’t even know how to swim.” He clutched at the sleeves of Marlow’s shirt, tears forming in his eyes, “I could’ve died.”

“Hey, hey. You’re fine.” Marlow cooed, cupping Mary’s face in his hands, “You’re fine. Half the crew doesn’t even know how to swim. You’ve got me though.” He ran his thumbs over their eyes, “I’m here for you. I won’t let any harm come your way.”

Mary looked up and noticed the sheer panic hiding behind the other’s eyes. For someone speaking so calmly to him, they seemed awfully worried about his well being. He found himself leaning into the touch, far too tired to even allow himself to protest.
And finally Marlow could breathe a sigh of relief. He couldn’t help but laugh, making little effort to move, “Do you feel ok to walk now? We need to get you into some dry clothes.” Mary protested, and Marlow tried to bite back his own laughter, “I can assure you I’ve probably seen everyone on the ship naked. I have enough decorum to make sure you’re not uncomfortable.”
