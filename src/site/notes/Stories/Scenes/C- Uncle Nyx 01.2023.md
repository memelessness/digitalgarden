Description: The one where Nyx reminisces about Mycan

**

“Uncle Nyx, have you ever been in love?”  
  
Nyx looked down to the small child that waited at their feet. They never knew how xe could be so bold as to approach them. Xir mother never bothered until much closer to her rule, “Once before.” They spoke flatly, leaning their weight to one side.

“What was that like?” The child looked with such expecting eyes, swaying with that childish lack of awareness.

Nyx let out a slow sigh, thinking about that time from long ago, “Like floating on air.” She thought about her bedroom, the only room of which no one else would dare enter. Thousands of treasures lined its walls, and yet the most valuable to all was the crown of ebony roses gifted to her when they finally reached their time of peace.

“And… there’s a sense of duty in it. That you know you will do anything to protect them and their legacy.” She looked down to the child, genuinely smiling for probably the first time in a little while, “Why? Do you think you may be in love?”  
  
The child scrunched xir face really hard at this response as if pondering the most difficult questions, “No, I don’t think so.”

They couldn’t help but laugh, “Well, what about your mothers?”

“... I don’t think I can marry my mothers?” This seemed to confuse xem.

“But there’s other kinds of love.” They knelt down to talk at xir level, “You wouldn’t want your mothers hurt, right?”  
  
“Well, no.”

“You want them to be happy, right?”  
  
“...Yeah, but.”

“Being in love doesn’t necessarily mean needing a romantic partner.” Nyx put a hand to xir shoulder as it was still clear xe was confused, “And it’s especially important as a ruler too.”

The child cocked their head to the side, watching Nyx with that starstruck wonder xir many-greats grandfather used to have, “Really?”

“Of course. You need to be able to look out for your people. To keep them safe from anyone that could bring the mountain harm.”They knew this kid was probably too young to understand, but they knew xe’d get there someday, “It’s why they’re such proud people. There’s so much love in the mountain, and that’s what has kept them strong.”

“But you’re my people too.” Xe spoke with profoundness, nodding xir head, “And you’re the strongest person I know! So that means you’re the most loved!”  
  
Nyx’s smile became a little pained, as if the years of being forgotten had finally caught up to them, but they decided to push it away for the sake of this precious little guy, “I’m afraid it doesn’t quite work that way.”  
  
“Well… then I’ll just make sure to love you the most.” Xe spoke very matter-of-factly, “And then it will be the case. Because I said so.”

And Nyx laughed, unable to control the little clouds of stardust that escaped above them, “I suppose your word is truth, little lordling.” They reached up to ruffle xir hair, watching as xe was enchanted by the little sparks settled to the ground. They could tell already that this little lord would be far better than the rest… Other than Mycan, of course.**
