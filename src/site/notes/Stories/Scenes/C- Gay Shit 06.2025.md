Description: The one where Cosmos and Aidoneus have a little heart to heart after a day in

**

And after a rather pleasant night in, Aidoneus and Cosmos found themselves sharing a bed together. She laid her head on his chest, limbs intertwined, breaths shared amongst each other in a secret melody.

Aid lifted up Cosmos’s hand to study. Gently, he’d press a thumb to her palm, and trustingly, her calloused fingers would comply with each movement.

Cosmos watched their fingers interlock. Occasionally, they would share these moments. Where something would linger in the air, taunting one another. Like a tense breath of air, hitching at the back of one’s throat. Before, neither of them would dare to break this quiet. This delicate little moment that could shatter in an instant.

“What’s on your mind.” Cosmos eventually spoke up, concern disguised as a command. 

But Aidoneus hummed a soft tone in response, pressing his lips to her knuckles, “Nothing you need to worry about my dear.” His voice was sweet, layered thick in a deceit that came naturally to him.

Cosmos craned her neck to face him, running a gentle finger against his cheek, “You can tell me.”

And there returned the silence. An impregnable recurrence to their relationship. Of words unspoken, and ignored for well over a century, “I wouldn’t want to ruin the mood.” He attempted to diffuse this line of questioning, carding a hand through her messy hair. 

Yet Cosmos was still determined, bringing an arm under her to lift herself properly, looking Aidoneus in the eye. Oh, how weak he was to those eyes, “We…” She started, suddenly losing her nerve. She’d, reluctantly, spoke to Moss about this before… but she knew she wasn’t the best at communicating. She had a lot more experience in making this sort of thing worse, “We don’t talk like we used to.” She settled on, trying to stay firm.

Aidoneus opened his mouth to speak, the words dying when he looked at her neck. A twisting scar down to her shoulder, reminding him of all the sorrow she’s had to face, “We don’t.” He agreed, looking back to her eyes.

The gaze hadn’t gone unnoticed to her as she shifted in the sheets, covering herself as she looked away.

“I’m sorry I-”

“No, I… just…”

And that made Aidoneus just feel terrible, “Cos, I didn’t mean to.” He reached up to her, gently ghosting his thumb against her cheek.

“No, I know it’s…” Unsightly, she wanted to say, but breathing air into such a thing was painful.

Aidoneus sat himself up, wrapping his arms around her. And with a heavy sigh, he buried his face into her neck, breathing her in for a moment, “I was thinking about when you left.” He muttered softly, tracing gentle shapes into the small of her back, “I wasn’t in a great place. I thought you’d died. And when you came back… I don’t know, it just took me a while to actually believe it. I’ve just been… so relieved lately to know you're safe. Knowing I can hold you again.”

Cosmos eventually returned the gesture, draping her arms over his shoulder, “That was my fault. I should’ve sent a letter or something.”

He shook his head, guiding her closer, “No, no. That could’ve been worse. Your…… ex was insane. I couldn’t bear it if things got worse because of me.”

Cosmos let out a slow sigh, moving her fingers through his long frizzy hair, “Valentine was the lowest point of my life.” She started, tracing her fingers to the part in his scalp, “But back then, I know I could come to you with my deepest problems… Sometimes I feel like I still can but… it’s….”

“Different.” He finished her sentence, taking in a deep breath, “I’ve noticed it too.”

“In some ways for the better?”

“How oddly optimistic of you.”

Cosmos looked up to the ceiling, watching how the intricate pattern swirled against the grain of the wood, “I loved you then too.”

Aidoneus then pulled away, a cocky grin against his face, “You know I kind of knew that.”

Cosmos smiled softly, the sarcasm swiftly stripping away from him, “But I’m glad we get to be together now? Despite the hardships? And… I’d like to be able to talk how we used to again? Sometime?”
