**The Coastal Underground Fight Ring** is located in the Capitol of the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Coast\|Coast]] where rare faerie folk are abducted and made to fight.

[[People/Pirates/Felix\|Felix]] is one of the few people who escaped.

[[People/Pirates/Mary\|Mary]] outlawed it as soon as he heard over it and destroyed the ring himself.
