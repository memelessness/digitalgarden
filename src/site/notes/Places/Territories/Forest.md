**The Forest** is a territory of the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Faerielands\|Faerielands]] and ruled by Highlord [[People/Highlords/Waldor\|Waldor]].


The Forest has remained one of the most neutral territories for millennia. This is because of [[People/Gods/The Well\|The Well]] that lives deep within the [[Enchanted Forest\|Enchanted Forest]]. It is a very sacred entity to the faerie kind, as it has the power to tell prophecies.
