**The Meadows** is a territory of the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Faerielands\|Faerielands]] and has no Highlord


A large portion of the Meadows were burnt down by [[Ideas/Events/Fire\|Fire]] some time ago, but for some it might as well have just been yesterday. It was once covered in plush grass and wildflowers, blessed to be one of few territories to retain its seasonal stasis of spring. There are some areas that are still green, but what little magic remains is only barely habitable.
