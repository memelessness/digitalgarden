**The Spark of Innovation** is the capitol city of the [[Places/Territories/Flats/Flats\|Flats]]. 


Founded by [[People/Changelings (There's a lot I'm so sorry)/Elouise Braveheart\|Elouise Braveheart]], now referred to as "The Architect"



A statue of Ra (The [[People/Gods/Sun\|Sun]]) is found at the city center. There stands a large figure, their hands lifted to the sky, eyes looking down to the observer. A wheel of thorns rotates around them. Two smaller statues are also seen. The middle is looking down to the mote that surrounds them, tears hanging from his eyes (The [[People/Gods/Moon\|Moon]]). The smallest is a figure with its hands clasped together. It has a third eye on its forehead, forever closed ([[People/Gods/The Well\|The Well]]). There is a sign talking about it. The name of the commissioner is carved out and illegible.

The Moon takes great care of the statue of The Well, as it is one of few depictions of it that remain. Sometimes, he'll sit at its feet and pray.
