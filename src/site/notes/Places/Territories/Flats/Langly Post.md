**The Langly Post** is a post office that used to be in charge of all the mail that was carried out through the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Faerielands\|Faerielands]]. It is located in [[Places/Territories/Flats/The Spark of Innovation\|The Spark of Innovation]] in the [[Places/Territories/Flats/Flats\|Flats]]. Since the downfall of [[Ideas/The Architect\|The Architect]], the Langly Post has become more of a Rookery for the ravens that carry the mail across the land.
