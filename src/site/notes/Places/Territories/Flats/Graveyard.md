**The Graveyard** is a site in the [[Places/Territories/Flats/Flats\|Flats]] where a bunch of old, powered down robots lay to rest at the foot of the [[Places/Territories/Ruins of Stars/Ruins of Stars\|Ruins of Stars]].

[[Dnd (Not Canon)/The Lamplighter\|The Lamplighter]] is a robot of the Graveyard, powered by magic of [[Dnd (Not Canon)/Mizzette\|Mizzette]] and the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Shadowlands\|Shadowlands]]
