**The Rumor Mill** is an organization run within [[Places/Territories/Oasis/The Bazaar\|The Bazaar]] of  the [[Places/Territories/Oasis/Oasis\|Oasis]]. Its primary focus is to collect and sell information to those who make a good offer

Consists of -
The Mind- Focuses on more scholarly information
The Heart- Focuses on the brothels
The Eyes- Focuses on a more subtle approach. Trained spies and assassins
