**The Oasis** is located in the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Faerielands\|Faerielands]] and is ruled by Highlord [[People/Highlords/Fable\|Fable]]


The Oasis is home to many scholars and merchants. The Bazaar is also located here, a wide-spreading market that’s open year-round, offering all sorts of goods from little food stalls to well-forged trinkets.




In the Oasis, there is [[Places/Territories/Oasis/The Library\|The Library]], and [[Places/Territories/Oasis/The Bazaar\|The Bazaar]]
