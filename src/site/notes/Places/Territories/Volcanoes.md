**The Volcanoes** are located in the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Faerielands\|Faerielands]] and ruled over by Highlord [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Valentine\|Valentine]] 


The Volcanoes are home to a very proud people. Not much wildlife lives up there, but due to its closeness to the water, the land provides an abundance of obsidian that has been used to create strong weapons
