**The Mountains** are a territory in the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Faerielands\|Faerielands]]

No one really knows much about the Mountains, other than what they see from the fearsome High Lords that have come down out of their obligation for peace. The mountains stretch far past the clouds, stuck in the torrential blizzard that had remained from the old winter court. Many have tried to scale it, and yet only the mountain Fae have returned.


Ruled under Highlord [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Cosmos\|Cosmos]] by Law, yet [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Moss\|Moss]] by Magic. Protected by [[People/NYX STORY RELEVENT/Nyx Snow\|Nyx Snow]]
