**The Red Woods** is located in the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Faerielands\|Faerielands]] and is ruled by Highlord [[People/Highlords/Umber\|Umber]].



The Red Woods have no exports, and are too stubborn for imports. They are a proud people of hunters and gatherers


Their lifestyle was slowly starting to fall apart as the need for trade increases (being in-between 2 major trade territories)


Under Highlord [[People/Highlords/Umber\|Umber]], The Red Woods started to charge traders to travel through the trade routes. Umber suspects the only reason their land isn't taken over is because they follow etiquette at [[Ideas/Events/Parties\|Parties]]
