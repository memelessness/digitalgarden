**The Court of Bones** is a Court found deep within the bones of the earth underneath the [[Places/Territories/Ruins of Stars/Ruins of Stars\|Ruins of Stars]]. The court had fallen long before the [[Places/Seasonal Courts/Seasonal Courts\|Seasonal Courts]] and remains as the faintest echo of what used to be.
