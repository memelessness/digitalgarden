**The Moon** (He/Him/His, They/Them/Theirs) is a Celestial Deity and one of the [[Ideas/Concepts/Old Gods\|Old Gods]]

Brother of the [[People/Gods/Sun\|Sun]] and [[People/Gods/The Well\|The Well]]

The Moon's magic smells like Watercress Lilies. Often depictions of the Moon consist of being overshadowed by the Sun (whose magic smells like smoke). Effigies of the moon are crescent-shaped with tears
