**The Sun** (They/Them/Theirs) is one of the constellation [[Ideas/Concepts/Old Gods\|Old Gods]]

The Sun's magic smells like smoke. Effigies of the sun often portray a starburst eye with a wheel of thorns


The Sun used to be the benefactor of [[People/Changelings (There's a lot I'm so sorry)/Elouise Braveheart\|Elouise Braveheart]], the architect of [[Places/Territories/Flats/The Spark of Innovation\|The Spark of Innovation]] before pulling the magic from her. They used the help of the [[People/Gods/Moon\|Moon]] and [[People/Gods/The Well\|The Well]] to spread rumors of Elouise and socially ostracize her from her own people.

The Sun is a current patron to [[People/Highlords/Osiris\|Osiris]], promising to reduce the severity of the floods in the [[Places/Territories/Canyons\|Canyons]].

The sun enjoys playing with people, either mortal or immortal. They tricked [[People/Gods/The Well\|The Well]] to go to the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Faerielands\|Faerielands]] after [[Ideas/Events/The Fracture\|The Fracture]], which resulted in it becoming trapped due to the people's desperation for magic. They tricked [[People/Changelings (There's a lot I'm so sorry)/Moxxie\|Moxxie]] into creating [[Places/Territories/Flats/The Spark of Innovation\|The Spark of Innovation]], a city which became dependent on the already weak magic of the land. They promised to help Osiris in her struggles to keep her people alive, which leads to a heavy drought of the canyons. They treat the [[People/Gods/Moon\|Moon]] as someone lesser, despite them both being the last remaining Thrones of their pantheon.


The Sun is the brother to the [[People/Gods/Moon\|Moon]] and [[People/Gods/The Well\|The Well]].
