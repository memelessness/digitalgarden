**The Well** (It/Its) is one of the Constellation [[Ideas/Concepts/Old Gods\|Old Gods]] that was trapped in a wishing well in the [[Places/Territories/Forest\|Forest]].

The Well's magic smells like a distant memory, depictions of it are oftentimes made with a third eye closed. Effigies are few and far between, although there is one in [[Places/Territories/Flats/The Spark of Innovation\|The Spark of Innovation]] at the statue of Ra

Having been deceived by its brothers the [[People/Gods/Sun\|Sun]] and [[People/Gods/Moon\|Moon]], it came down to the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Faerielands\|Faerielands]] to share a great prophecy. As this happened after [[Ideas/Events/The Fracture\|The Fracture]], denizens were desperate for connection to [[Ideas/Concepts/Magic\|Magic]] and trapped it in hopes to have access to its magic. This did not work and The Well had been trapped ever since.

After [[Places/Territories/Territories\|Territories]] were established in the Faerielands, fae folk sought The Well once more and it decided to share its gift of [[Ideas/Concepts/Prophecy\|Prophecy]] again.


The Well, has recruited [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Aidoneus\|Aidoneus]] to talk to it, as its sense of prophecy does not give it the full picture.
