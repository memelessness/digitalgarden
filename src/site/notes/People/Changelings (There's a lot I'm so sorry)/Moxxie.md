**Moxxie** (It/Its) is a [[Ideas/Races/Changeling\|Changeling]] of the [[Places/Territories/Flats/Flats\|Flats]]. In this form, they take the visage of a small bird-like [[Ideas/Concepts/Lesser Faerie\|Lesser Faerie]]. It is obsessed with finding ways to get more magic to extend its lifespan. 

Moxxie (Formerly known as [[People/Changelings (There's a lot I'm so sorry)/Elouise Braveheart\|Elouise Braveheart]]) was born in the Flats after the events of [[Ideas/Events/The Fracture\|The Fracture]] had finally settled and before the boom in innovation erupted, often finding itself wandering the [[Places/Territories/Flats/Graveyard\|Graveyard]] up north in search for parts to work on its infant inventions. 

Upon the first signs of aging, Moxxie was greeted by the [[People/Gods/Sun\|Sun]] and struck up a deal. They would give it all the magic it could ever desire, so long as Moxxie promised to keep pursuing its inventions.

Moxxie founded the company [[Ideas/Concepts/Braveheart Magica\|Braveheart Magica]] and helped build [[Places/Territories/Flats/The Spark of Innovation\|The Spark of Innovation]] during its high life era. Its skill in [[Ideas/Concepts/Magitech\|Magitech]] became nothing short of impressive, its products even becoming a household name across the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Faerielands\|Faerielands]]. Unfortunately, this wealth of magic didn't last forever.

Without notice, the Sun revoked Moxxie's magic, and the supply quickly began to run dry. As a result, Braveheart Magica was forced to reroute any Magitech through the land itself to keep its power supply. Other [[Places/Territories/Territories\|Territories]] had no problem with this, however, the [[Places/Territories/Flats/Flats\|Flats]] took a really heavy toll with how much magic the city requires. Since then, The Spark of Innovation has spent a lot of its time developing generators that can transfer to Magitech. In the meantime, the Flats have struggled even more to give its denizens the magic they need to survive.

Moxxie's name and brand was reduced to a story told to warn children. Its decent from stardom, from riches to rags, would continue on for many years to come. Ever since then, Moxxie has been fighting to extend its lifespan, even going so far as to steal magic from others.

After struggling for magic, Moxxie came up with a plan to steal the magic for itself once more, disguising itself as a mortal and taking on the alias [[People/Changelings (There's a lot I'm so sorry)/Madryck Roslof\|Madryck Roslof]]. Through this means, it was able to become a warlock to [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Guinevere\|Guinevere]]. This didn't last forever, as the magic ran out fast after her passing. 

It currently get its magic supply by scavenging old magical artifacts, but it's found a recent obsession with the abundance of untethered magic it can find in the Shadowlands, and this quest is starting to make it desperate as it starts to age again.

Moxxie takes a great interest in the [[Ideas/Concepts/Magic\|Magic]] of the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Shadowlands\|Shadowlands]] and holds a very firm belief that radio static has a direct connection to the realm.


Moxxie has developed arthritis  majorly in its hands and minorly in its hips and shoulders, which flares up any time its weened from magic. The pain ebbs away when its able to store enough magic (which isn't common, as it's basically living off of scraps, but every few years it's able to stumble across something more sustainable).

If it can't get magic (which it's found enough methods to at least delay the inevitable), it begins to rely more on mobility aids. It keeps a cane by the door of its condo just in case they have a heavier paid day.
