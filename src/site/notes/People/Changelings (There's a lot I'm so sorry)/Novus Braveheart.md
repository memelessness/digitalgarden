**Novus** (Any/All) is a [[Ideas/Races/Changeling\|Changeling]] who grew up in the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Mortal Realm\|Mortal Realm]]. Their surrogate parents are Goblins (last name formerly Bone Grinder)

Novus grew up using their shapeshifting abilities to steal for their family.


Novus is married to [[People/Changelings (There's a lot I'm so sorry)/Dianne Braveheart\|Dianne Braveheart]]
