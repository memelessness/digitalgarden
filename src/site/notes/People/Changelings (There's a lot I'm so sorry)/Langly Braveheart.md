**Langly** (He/Him/His) is the middle child of the Braveheart siblings. He was born before [[Ideas/Events/The Fracture\|The Fracture]] and was raise by his brother [[People/Changelings (There's a lot I'm so sorry)/Conrad Braveheart\|Conrad Braveheart]]. During the war, they escaped the [[Places/Seasonal Courts/Court of Stars\|Court of Stars]] and fled to the [[Places/Territories/Flats/Flats\|Flats]].



When [[Places/Territories/Flats/The Spark of Innovation\|The Spark of Innovation]] was created, he became in charge of the [[Places/Territories/Flats/Langly Post\|Langly Post]]


Sibling of [[People/Changelings (There's a lot I'm so sorry)/Elouise Braveheart\|Elouise Braveheart]] and [[People/Changelings (There's a lot I'm so sorry)/Conrad Braveheart\|Conrad Braveheart]]
