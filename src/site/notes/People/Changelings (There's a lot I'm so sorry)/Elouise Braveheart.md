**Elouise** (She/Her/Hers, He/Him/His, They/Them/Theirs, It/Its) is a [[Ideas/Races/Changeling\|Changeling]] of the [[Places/Territories/Flats/Flats\|Flats]] and the youngest of 3 siblings. She takes on the visage of a cuckoo bird.


Elouise's mother lived in the [[Places/Seasonal Courts/Court of Stars\|Court of Stars]] as a [[Ideas/Concepts/Court-less Faerie\|Court-less Faerie]] and ran to the Flats during [[Ideas/Events/The Fracture\|The Fracture]]. Elouise's older brothers grew up during the war and recall stories of their mother needing to sleep around just to keep her children fed (Which resulted in Elouise's birth).

Elouise only really cared about her inventions as she got older, her brothers often scolding her for not pulling her own weight to help them survive.

After making a deal with the [[People/Gods/Sun\|Sun]], they were able to invent [[Ideas/Concepts/Magitech\|Magitech]] and used it as the foundation to build [[Places/Territories/Flats/The Spark of Innovation\|The Spark of Innovation]] and 


(Want the Sun to be an abusive shit. Maybe convinced El to abuse drugs. Nauxia)




When her power was revoked, the city resorted into stealing magic from the already struggling land, which made it much more difficult for the Flats to support life as the other territories would. This damage resulted in her being scorned by her people.

She lost her home, her family, her company, and decided to go into hiding, becoming [[People/Changelings (There's a lot I'm so sorry)/Moxxie\|Moxxie]]
