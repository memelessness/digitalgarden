**Mycan** (He/Him/His) is a [[Ideas/Concepts/Lesser Faerie\|Lesser Faerie]] of the [[Places/Seasonal Courts/Winter Court\|Winter Court]] and founder of the [[Places/Territories/Mountains\|Mountains]]


Married to [[People/NYX STORY RELEVENT/Lily\|Lily]], father of 3, and friend of [[People/NYX STORY RELEVENT/Nyx Snow\|Nyx Snow]]

Served under [[People/NYX STORY RELEVENT/Noran Aldrich\|Noran Aldrich]] and Lieutenant to the Winter Court Military 


#### From Jeremiah's Vault
Mycan was a high-ranking official in the Winter kingdom's army, one of [[People/NYX STORY RELEVENT/Nyx Snow\|Nyx Snow]]'s superiors. Ran into her occasionally prior to the fracture. He's a mixture of lower fae and higher fae, which is a shitty way to be in SOCIETY, but he passes enough in the courts of the elven fae to make it as far as he has. He got to hear about the warning signs of the fracture in advance and began planning out his refuge society in the mountains for those that would need an escape from the fighting and the destruction that was surely going to come from the fracture. 

Has a few interactions with Nyx and, perhaps after a particularly awful interaction where Nyx's father berated her again, he offers Nyx a place on the mountains. She promises to think on this.

Due to his mixed blood, he does suffer a lot of abuse, even being as favored as he is. Noran puts a LOT on his plate due to his competency and the fact that he's low-born. The price for his sterling reputation is the expectations and pressure of the winter lord, and he's desperate to get the FUCK outta there. 

Mycan leaves as soon as his platoon is deployed to fight in the fractures. They go AWOL and are never heard from again. They collect the civilians and the other deserters on the way to the mountains. 

Nyx joins them shortly with her own band of men. Mycan, Nyx, and Mycan's future partner(s) work together to set up a society in the mountains and defend the territory, as well as coming up with the plan to scare people off from fucking with them. 

Mycan enlists Nyx as a member of his court, in their own special position with its own particular definition. She will perform this duty for millennia after he is gone. 

He has a bunch of kids that create the family tree where [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Moss\|Moss]] and [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Kettle\|Kettle]] stem from. 

Mycan discovers Nyx is the child of the [[People/NYX STORY RELEVENT/Noran Aldrich\|Noran Aldrich]] due to the Winter Lord assigning him guard duty on a trip to the Starry Court, which has requested evidence that Nyx is... alive. The Winter Lord spends the previous months leading up to this manipulating Mycan, especially pressing on that Mycan is like a son to him, to try and ensure Mycan's silence after overhearing/witnessing evidence that Nyx and the Winter Lord are related. 

FROM RP

Mycan is personally tasked by the Winter Lord to closely look over Nyx on missions and making sure she doesn't do anything. This leads in to the manipulation to get him to take Nyx to the starry court with him.
