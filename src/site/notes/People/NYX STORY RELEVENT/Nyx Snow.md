**Nyx** (They/Them/Theirs, She/Her/Hers) is a [[Ideas/Concepts/Lesser Faerie\|Lesser Faerie]] of the [[Places/Seasonal Courts/Autumn Court\|Autumn Court]] who changed her allegiances to the [[Places/Seasonal Courts/Winter Court\|Winter Court]] and later the [[Places/Territories/Mountains\|Mountains]].

Nyx's magic smells like Citrus and Sage

Formerly known as Nexus


Daughter of [[People/NYX STORY RELEVENT/Katrine Mathers\|Katrine Mathers]] and [[People/NYX STORY RELEVENT/Noran Aldrich\|Noran Aldrich]]

After her mother's death, Nyx stood before the [[Places/Seasonal Courts/Court of Stars\|Court of Stars]] to proclaim her lineage to the Winter King. She was able to accomplish this with the help of [[People/NYX STORY RELEVENT/Morcanan Quill\|Morcanan Quill]] and [[People/NYX STORY RELEVENT/Leif Fairchild\|Leif Fairchild]]







After [[People/NYX STORY RELEVENT/Mycan\|Mycan]] passed, she continued her allegiance to the Mountains, gathering intel through methods like the [[Places/Territories/Oasis/Rumor Mill\|Rumor Mill]]


After [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Cosmos\|Cosmos]]'s magic was cursed, Nyx continued to attend [[Ideas/Events/Parties\|Parties]] with her to bring protection.

Nyx is the surrogate uncle of [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Moss\|Moss]]
