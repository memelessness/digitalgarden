**Terra** (She/Her/Hers) is a silver [[Ideas/Races/Kitsune\|Kitsune]] [[Ideas/Concepts/Lesser Faerie\|Lesser Faerie]] of the [[Places/Territories/Red Woods\|Red Woods]]

She serves as the body guard to Highlord [[People/Highlords/Umber\|Umber]]

Terra's magic smells like Magnolia Blossoms

Terra was blessed by the [[People/Gods/Moon\|Moon]] as a baby, and was later saved by him when her parents were killed by hunters. She hadn't heard from him since, but spent a lot of her young childhood praying at the [[Ideas/Concepts/God's Tree\|God's Tree]].

After the hunt of her parents, the Moon brought Terra to the village Umber lived where she learned the way of the blade and took arms. They met one night at a festival where they both were there to entertain. They became best friends and essentially sisters after that.

She is the mother of [[People/Pirates/Felix\|Felix]] and [[People/Kit\|Kit]] and the sister of Highlord [[People/Highlords/Umber\|Umber]]
