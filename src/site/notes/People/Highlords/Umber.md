**Umber** (They/Them/Theirs) is a cat-like [[Ideas/Concepts/Lesser Faerie\|Lesser Faerie]] and Highlord of the [[Places/Territories/Red Woods\|Red Woods]].

Umber's magic smells like nectarines

They are essentially sisters to [[People/Terra\|Terra]] and the aunt of [[People/Pirates/Felix\|Felix]] and [[People/Kit\|Kit]]


At a young age, they were raised  to be raised as a Geisha. When the previous Highlord passed, the magic chose them to lead. This lead to [[Ideas/Events/The Exodus\|The Exodus]]
