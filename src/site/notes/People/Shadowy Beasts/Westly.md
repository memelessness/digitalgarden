**Westly** (He/Him/His) is a shadow demon of the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Shadowlands\|Shadowlands]]. He is shapeless and needs to take control of a body to navigate.

Has a shop at [[Places/Territories/Oasis/The Bazaar\|The Bazaar]] but is also able to be summoned when called upon. Deals in magical items
