**Felix** (He/Him/His) is a [[Ideas/Races/Kitsune\|Kitsune]] [[Ideas/Concepts/Lesser Faerie\|Lesser Faerie]] of the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Coast\|Coast]]


As a young pup, he was taken from his mother [[People/Terra\|Terra]] and became a spectacle of the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Coastal Underground Fight Ring\|Coastal Underground Fight Ring]] where he lost his leg.



He snuck onto the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Santa Antonio\|Santa Antonio]] to escape the ring where he was found by [[People/Pirates/Aiden\|Aiden]]. The crew allowed him to stay and he grew to have a fond, almost fatherly connection to [[People/Pirates/Milo\|Milo]].
