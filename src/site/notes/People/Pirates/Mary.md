**Mary** (He/Him/His, They/Them/Theirs) is the [[Ideas/Concepts/High Faerie\|High Faerie]] Highlord of the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Coast\|Coast]]


Mary joined the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Santa Antonio\|Santa Antonio]] to run away from his responsibilities as successor to [[People/Racist Father\|Racist Father]]. After adventuring for some time, he returned to the Coast to continue his responsibilities. 

Mary's initial reception on the ship is less than favorable; despite not aligning himself with his father's ideals, he still carries a lot of societal biases from being raised in the rich people environment 


He was married to [[People/Pirates/Marlow\|Marlow]] and had a child named [[People/Highlords/A Child\|A Child]]
