**Aiden** (He/Him/His) is a [[Ideas/Races/Changeling\|Changeling]] originally of the [[Places/Territories/Oasis/Oasis\|Oasis]] but later changed his allegiances to the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Coast\|Coast]] upon [[People/Pirates/Mary\|Mary]]'s coronation. 

He was raised as a human in the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Mortal Realm\|Mortal Realm]] and discovered he was a changeling at an older age. When his village kicked him out, he sought refuge in the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Faerielands\|Faerielands]] where he made a pact with the [[Places/Territories/Oasis/Rumor Mill\|Rumor Mill]] at [[Places/Territories/Oasis/The Bazaar\|The Bazaar]] 

He was the first of the crew to be saved by [[People/Pirates/Marlow\|Marlow]] and made a home out of the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Santa Antonio\|Santa Antonio]] where he spent many years as the chef.

He is married to [[People/Pirates/Milo\|Milo]] 

Aiden had an initial distaste of [[People/Pirates/Mary\|Mary]] when he joined the crew due to many pointedly racist comments toward his crew and husband but when Mary learned through his own biases, they were able to become great friends.

Aiden's ability to work with cursed objects makes him an essential member of the Santa Antonio.
