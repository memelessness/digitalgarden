**Ziggy (he/him/his)** is the son of a high fae line the mixed in with lower fae a generation or two back. Living in the capital of the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Coast\|Coast]], Ziggy is eventually hired as the castle baker under [[People/Pirates/Mary\|Mary]]'s rule, and works a bit at a bakery near the docks when he's got the extra time. 

He's a distant relation of Shea's-- though she belongs to the more well-off part of the family that inherited the land outside the city. She's one of the few people that cares to acknowledge his connection to her family,  and catches up with him whenever they get the chance. 

He eventually meets [[People/Pirates/Felix\|Felix]], when Felix has reconnected with Mary. They eventually begin dating.
