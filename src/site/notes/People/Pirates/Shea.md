**Shea (she/her/her's):** One of many children of a minor noble family outside of the capital of the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Coast\|Coast]], Shea manages the inventory and local deliveries of her family's pearl business, making a brisk trade with the Castle in the era of [[People/Racist Father\|Racist Father]] and [[People/Pirates/Mary\|Mary]]'s rule. It's not exactly what she's into, but she gets it done, and is decently on her way to a good portion of inheritance. 

During her visits to the castle, she runs across Ryan, a man in charge of childcare on the castle grounds. They encounter each other often, and eventually start to date, regardless of their difference in class and positions in the racial hierarchy. 

They eventually have three children. 

Shea is a distant relative of [[People/Pirates/Ziggy\|Ziggy]]'s. His side of the family was removed from the inheritance and emotionally distanced from Shea's line, due to a marriage into a lower fae family, but she still holds that tie as important. She keeps relatively close contact with him whenever she gets the chance.
