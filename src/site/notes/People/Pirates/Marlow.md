**Marlow** (He/Him/His) is a Water Genasi of the [[Places/Territories/Grasslands\|Grasslands]] 



As a young child, his family made [[Ideas/Events/Pilgrimage\|Pilgrimage]] from the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Ocean\|Ocean]] to the [[Places/Territories/Grasslands\|Grasslands]].

When he got older, he decided to join a pirate ship and return to the Ocean in his own way. Overtime, he was eventually able to become the Captain of the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Santa Antonio\|Santa Antonio]], though he insisted to be treated as an equal


Marlow was essential to the ship, notably saving [[People/Pirates/Aiden\|Aiden]] and [[People/Pirates/Izzie\|Izzie]] from particularly difficult home situations. He also made great friends with [[People/Pirates/Milo\|Milo]]

Marlow and Izzy came to play music together on the ship, him slowly becoming more vulnerable and openly lamenting about his childhood in the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Ocean\|Ocean]] and how he was never able to learn his parents' native tongue due to the increasing need for trade and the eventual more to the mainland.

He met [[People/Pirates/Mary\|Mary]] at a bar in the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Coast\|Coast]], inviting him aboard their ship.


He later eloped with Mary, settling down at the Coast in a beachside home with their daughter [[People/Highlords/A Child\|A Child]]

Marlow was later murdered by [[People/Racist Father\|Racist Father]]
