**Ryan (he/him/his):** A lower fae werewolf-adjacent man who has a special talent for early childhood care after spending his early life with inconsistent guardians. Using his lack of paper trail proving his racial status, Ryan saves up for a high-quality glamor, and moves to the capital city of the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Coast\|Coast]] to get work. 

He's hired as the primary caretaker of [[People/Racist Father\|Racist Father]]'s child [[People/Pirates/Mary\|Mary]] when the child is relatively young, and plays a huge part in the child's development. They bond very closely, with Ryan taking a very active role in Mary's emotional development and education, often taking Mary out on trips to see and understand the city that Mary would someday be making so many decisions for. 

Ryan is eventually discovered to be a lower fae and dramatically removed from his post as Mary's caretaker. Despite Mary's objections, and the long history of quality care, Mary's father threatens and fearmongers Ryan before demoting him to a far more obscure and lesser-paying job, threatening to expose Ryan as a lower fae to any potential employer in the city if Ryan sought work elsewhere. Told in no uncertain terms not to make himself known to Mary again, Ryan reluctantly tries to lose himself in his new work with the children of the servants, and not worry too badly about the young child he'd nurtured. 

It's working this job where he meets [[People/Pirates/Shea\|Shea]], local minor noble and pearl trader, and falls in love with her, courting her as best as he's able around the strict eye of Mary's father and the curious minds of his charges. 

Eventually, when Mary overthrows his father's position, Mary rediscovers Ryan and reconnects with him. Ryan learns about [[People/Highlords/A Child\|A Child]], and is charged with being her caretaker as Mary handles his new duties. He also is there for Mary as Mary mourns the passing of [[People/Pirates/Marlow\|Marlow]], as well as the actions he had to take against his father. 

Ryan also provides guidance for [[People/Pirates/Felix\|Felix]], when Felix begins coming around to visit.
