**Milo** (He/Him/His) is a [[Naga\|Naga]] [[Ideas/Concepts/Lesser Faerie\|Lesser Faerie]] of the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Coast\|Coast]].


He joined the crew of the [[Places/Territories/Coast/Santa Antonio\|Santa Antonio]] where he met his best friend [[People/Pirates/Marlow\|Marlow]] and [[People/Pirates/Aiden\|Aiden]], the love of his life.

Milo is married to [[People/Pirates/Aiden\|Aiden]] and the surrogate father of [[People/Pirates/Felix\|Felix]]
