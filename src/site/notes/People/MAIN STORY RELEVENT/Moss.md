**Moss** (Xe/Xem/Xir) is the [[Ideas/Concepts/Lesser Faerie\|Lesser Faerie]] Highlord of the [[Places/Territories/Mountains\|Mountains]] in magic only.

Moss's Magic smells like Basil


Xe gave [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Cosmos\|Cosmos]] the title of Highlord so she could deal with the more social requirements of leadership while xe lead the military



Moss is the son of [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Kettle\|Kettle]] and [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Guinevere\|Guinevere]] and married to [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Cosmos\|Cosmos]], [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Artemis\|Artemis]], and [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Aidoneus\|Aidoneus]]. Xe is also the grandson of [[People/Highlords/Fable\|Fable]] and a cousin to [[People/Ainsley\|Ainsley]]
