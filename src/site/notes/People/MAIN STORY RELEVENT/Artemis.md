**Artemis** (She/Her/Hers) is a human that wandered into the [[Places/Territories/Mountains\|Mountains]]

Born one of many children to a woodsman in an isolated forest cottage, Artemis spends most of her early life learning her role in the survival of her family, isolated from almost all other humans. She picks up a knack for hunting, and takes the role as the family's primary hunter as a way to both continue to earn her place and get as much distance from the house as possible. While the distance and quiet allows her to regulate, the task doesn't allow her to fully focus on parsing how she feels like she doesn't belong among her family. 

This is what eventually brings her path to cross with [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Moss\|Moss]], in their animal form, who leads her into the Mountains, and promptly leaves her to be discovered by [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Cosmos\|Cosmos]]. Before Cosmos manages to find her, however, she's almost consumed by creatures of the Mountain. Cosmos interrupts, and tells the mountain denizens that they can't hurt Artemis, as they're betrothed, and any act against Artemis would be an act against their Lord.
