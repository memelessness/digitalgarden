**Kettle** (She/Her/Hers) is a [[Ideas/Concepts/Lesser Faerie\|Lesser Faerie]] and Highlord of the [[Places/Territories/Mountains\|Mountains]]

Helped give [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Cosmos\|Cosmos]] some encouragement after running away from home


Wife of [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Guinevere\|Guinevere]], Mother of [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Moss\|Moss]] and descendent of [[People/NYX STORY RELEVENT/Mycan\|Mycan]]

Unlike her son, she grew up afraid of [[People/NYX STORY RELEVENT/Nyx Snow\|Nyx Snow]]
