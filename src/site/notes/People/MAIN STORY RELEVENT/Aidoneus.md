**Aidoneus** (He/Him/His, She/Her/Hers) is a [[Ideas/Concepts/High Faerie\|High Faerie]] of the [[Places/Territories/Forest\|Forest]]


Aidoneus at a young age was coaxed by [[People/Gods/The Well\|The Well]] to drink its water. This has given him the gift of lying without consequence. In return, he spends a lot of his time gathering intel on other royal families and governmental happenings to inform it of news happening in the [[Places/Planes of Existance/Faerielands\|Faerielands]]

Aidoneus is the son of [[People/Highlords/Waldor\|Waldor]], brother of [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Briar Rose\|Briar Rose]], and husband to [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Artemis\|Artemis]], [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Cosmos\|Cosmos]], and [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Moss\|Moss]]

#### From Jeremiah's Vault

Aidoneus is my sweet sweet existential stoner boy and I love him, ok? He is the understanding, open, so cunty, so everything. 

I think that he's used to being written off. He drinks the well water at like, nine at the youngest, eleven at the oldest. I think from then on out, his parents don't have high hopes for him. I don't know that they ever tell people what Aid did to gain favor with the well, but EVERYONE knows that whatever Aid did, it was for sure wicked unorthodox. Not EXACTLY blasphemous, obviously, since Aid didn't get banished, but there's this brand of "other" put on Aid because of this. 

I don't think he talks to Briar much. I think Briar is much too old and far to into whatever she's doing to be close to him at all. When she's home, she's super busy doing stuff for their parents, and then she leaves to go backpacking across the faery realm, so he doesn't ever super get her to back him up. 

But there's still hope for Aidoneus, in his parents' eyes. He gets a bit older, still generally playing nice, and then he gets his prophecy
