**Cosmos** (She/Her/Hers) is the [[Ideas/Concepts/High Faerie\|High Faerie]] Highlord of the [[Places/Territories/Mountains\|Mountains]] 

Cosmos' magic smells like Orange Blossoms

Cosmos was originally supposed to be the successor of the [[Places/Territories/Meadows\|Meadows]] but was made to run after the [[Ideas/Events/Fire\|Fire]]




Cosmos' [[Ideas/Concepts/Prophecy\|Prophecy]] lead her to breakup with her fiancé [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Valentine\|Valentine]], which lead to the burning of the [[Places/Territories/Meadows\|Meadows]]

Cosmos is married to [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Artemis\|Artemis]], [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Aidoneus\|Aidoneus]], and [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Moss\|Moss]]
