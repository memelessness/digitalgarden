**Ainsley** (ne/nem/ner) is a moth-boy [[Ideas/Concepts/Lesser Faerie\|Lesser Faerie]] of the [[Places/Territories/Oasis/Oasis\|Oasis]]

At a young age, Ainsley was adopted into the royal family of the [[Places/Territories/Oasis/Oasis\|Oasis]]. Ne grew up believing ner clearly autistic troubles were due to nem being lesser fae

Ainsley took over the family home upon ner adoptive parents' passing where ne met [[People/The Handler\|The Handler]]. 


Ainsley is cousins to [[People/MAIN STORY RELEVENT/Moss\|Moss]] and adoptive nephew to [[People/Highlords/Fable\|Fable]]
